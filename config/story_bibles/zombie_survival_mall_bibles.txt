Dark charcoal or navy work shirt.

Important Props:

4. CINEMATIC STYLE BIBLE
Overall Visual Identity

Genre: Realistic survival horror documentary.
Visual Approach: Photorealistic, grounded, atmospheric, and believable.
The world should resemble an actual modern shopping mall experiencing a prolonged catastrophic emergency.

Avoid:
Fantasy environments.
Futuristic technology.
Exaggerated monsters.
Cartoon aesthetics.
Superhero-style characters.
Overly stylized video-game environments.
Unnecessary dramatic effects.

Lighting Progression:
Night 1: Fluorescent commercial lighting, followed by sudden darkness.
Days 3–15: Flashlight illumination, weak emergency lights, and limited ambient light.
Days 19–42: Deep shadows, occasional lightning, flashlight beams, and red flare illumination.
Days 51–57: Violent storm lighting, skylight illumination, and rain entering the mall.
Days 58–60: Very dark survival base contrasted with the ruined, dimly illuminated mall below.

Color Progression:
Normal mall: Neutral whites, beige, commercial lighting.
Early outbreak: Cold grey, dark blue, black.
Underground: Concrete grey, dirty brown, muted green.
Sporting goods base: Dark grey, muted green, dusty brown.
Flare scenes: Deep red, black.
Storm scenes: Cold blue, charcoal, rain grey.
Final scenes: Desaturated grey, dark brown, muted natural colors.

Composition Variety:
Wide architectural establishing shots.
Medium character shots.
Close-ups of important objects.
Overhead mall layouts.
Surveillance-camera perspectives.
First-person observational perspectives.
Mezzanine viewpoints.
Underground tunnel perspectives.
Environmental destruction shots.
Intimate character moments.

Avoid repeating the same composition in consecutive visuals.
