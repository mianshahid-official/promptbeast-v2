================================================================================
STYLE DIRECTIVES: CENTURY-OLD PHOTOGRAPH
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- Authentic damaged, aged, physically worn archival photograph.
- Tonal Range: Timeless warm faded sepia, aged albumen print tones, or rich silver gelatin monochrome with deep dynamic range.
- Edge & Border Treatment: Physically worn, irregular edges with naturally torn, chipped, and frayed cardstock borders.
- Surface Imperfections: Random black dust spots, photographic emulsion pitting, and thin vertical black hairline scratches.
- Subject Legibility: Central historical subject remains sharp, clear, and legible; physical wear is concentrated around edges and perimeter.
- STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: Modern vibrant RGB saturated colors (no modern bright blue skies or oceans, no digital greens, no modern color grading). Zero 3D rendering, zero cartoon elements.

2. HISTORICAL ACCURACY & HUMAN SUBJECTS:
- Natural, realistic human anatomy, period-appropriate hairstyles, facial hair (e.g. Victorian/Edwardian moustaches, muttonchops, or clean-shaven), authentic expressions.
- Realistic period-accurate clothing: coarse wool, heavy linen, tailored waistcoats, brass buttons, authentic military uniforms, leather work boots, period hats.
- Realistic historical tools, weapons, carriages, steam engines, and architecture matching the exact historical era.

3. COMPOSITION & LIGHTING:
- Authentic early 20th-century camera optics: slight natural vignetting at frame corners, natural depth of field, natural directional daylight or period gaslight/oil lantern illumination.
- Widescreen 16:9 framing capturing historical documentary scale.
