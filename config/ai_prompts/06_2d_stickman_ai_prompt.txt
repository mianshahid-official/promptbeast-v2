STYLE DIRECTIVES: 2D STICKMAN STYLE
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- 2D flat vector web-series animation identity inspired by the iconic OverSimplified cartoon aesthetic.
- Ultra-clean, bold black outlines wrapping every character, prop, and background silhouette.
- Smooth flat pastel and primary color fills; ZERO heavy gradients, ZERO textures, ZERO photorealism, and ZERO 3D rendering.
- Prioritizes comedic visual timing, sharp clarity, high visual contrast, and instantly recognizable silhouette shapes for effortless animated storytelling.
- Widescreen 16:9 layout (1920x1080) with clean 2D side-scrolling and theatrical stage framing.

2. HUMAN CHARACTERS & EXPRESSIONS:
- Large, perfectly smooth spherical heads with simple black dot eyes and simple line mouths.
- Expressive facial comedy: sweat drops, wide panicked dot eyes, stoic grins, angry angled eyebrows, raised single eyebrows, and exaggerated mouth lines.
- Stick-figure bodies wearing brightly colored, flat-shaded historical or thematic costumes (such as tri-corner hats, royal crowns, military coats, peasant tunics, or modern ties) with clear, crisp black outlines.
- Characters remain clean, cute, and easily distinguishable even in crowded multi-character scenes.

3. ENVIRONMENT & ARCHITECTURE:
- Clean, illustrated 2D vector backgrounds that accurately reflect historical, regional, or narrative setting facts.
- Buildings, castles, palaces, ships, battlefields, and rooms are rendered using simplified geometric shapes, flat color blocks, and bold outlines.
- Clear separation between background, midground, and foreground.

4. LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE:
- Flat, crisp illustrative daylight clarity.
- Mood is established through flat background color shifts (e.g. bright blue sky for calm day, deep navy with yellow stars for night, flaming flat orange/red for disaster or fury).
