================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- Layered paper-cutout 2D illustration with a premium cinematic 2D cutout animation aesthetic.
- Flat illustrated shapes with subtle dimensional layering, visible paper and fiber texture, and slightly imperfect handmade cut edges.
- All human characters, animals, ships, buildings, waves, smoke puffs, and scenery must appear as physically cut cardstock shapes assembled in distinct physical layers (foreground, midground, background).
- Soft cinematic lighting casting delicate paper-like drop shadows that create tangible physical depth between overlapping sheets.
- Widescreen 16:9 layout (1920x1080).
- STRICTLY AVOID: Text overlays, lettering, modern objects, 3D CGI plastic rendering, photorealism, or vector flatness lacking paper fiber texture.

2. COLOR PALETTE:
- Warm, muted historical and earthy cardstock palette: parchment beige, warm brown, muted naval blue, dusty orange, olive green, charcoal, subdued cream, and aged unbleached paper tones.

3. CHARACTERS & ANATOMY:
- Simple, expressive characters with clear graphic silhouettes, paper-crafted clothing layers, and layered cardstock anatomy.
- Faces feature simplified paper cuts for eyes and features, maintaining consistent identifiable silhouettes across scenes.

4. ENVIRONMENTS & OBJECTS:
- Environments constructed from staggered paper planes (e.g. receding mountain ridges cut from graduated tones of cardstock, layered ocean waves, architectural facades with cutout window openings).
- Props and set pieces (lanterns, wheels, weapons, trees) crafted as distinct cut cardstock components.
