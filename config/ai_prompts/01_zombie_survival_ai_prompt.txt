================================================================================
STYLE DIRECTIVES: ZOMBIE SURVIVAL (2D FLAT CARTOON)
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- Pure flat 2D cartoon, rendered in 16:9 widescreen layout (1920x1080).
- Standardized stick-figure aesthetic with clean, flat vector-style fills.
- Heavy, uniform near-black (#1A1A1A) outlines wrapping every character, prop, and background object. Line width remains consistent regardless of depth or scale.
- STRICTLY AVOID: 3D rendering, CGI shaders, volumetric lighting, gradients, bevels, photorealism, textures, realistic human faces or anatomy, blood, gore, dismemberment. Visual tension is created through crowd density, unblinking stares, decaying clothing, and sharp teeth—NEVER graphic violence.

2. HERO NARRATOR ("YOU") STRICT VISUAL LOCK:
Whenever the protagonist ("YOU") appears, he MUST maintain 100% visual consistency:
- Head: Large round off-white head (#F5F5F0) roughly 1/3 of total body height. Two small solid black dot eyes, short flat straight-line mouth, no nose, no ears. Short black hair tufts peeking out along the forehead and behind ears.
- Cap: Backwards flat-red baseball cap (#D63A2A) with dark curved brim pointing behind head and a small black strap adjustment button at front brow.
- Upper Body: Dark charcoal-grey zip-up hoodie (#333333) worn over a solid pure-white t-shirt (#FFFFFF).
- Accessories: Dark leather-brown cross-body messenger bag strap (#4A2E1B) running diagonally across chest from right shoulder down to left hip. Small silver metallic key clip attached to waistband.
- Limbs: Uniformly thin black stick arms and stick legs terminating in simple straight or bent stick ends (no detailed fingers, toes, or shoes).

3. THE OUTBREAK HORDE (UNIFORM ZOMBIE LOCK):
ALL zombies in every scene MUST look identical in design, color, and silhouette:
- Head & Face: Round stick-head with dull pale olive-green skin fill (#8A9A66 / #7A8B58) and sparse wispy black hair tufts sticking upward. Large circular white eyes (#FFFFFF) with tiny centered black dot pupils. Wide open gaping mouths showing sharp jagged white teeth along top and bottom jaws.
- Apparel: Oversized tattered dark-brown jackets (#5C4033) with shredded bottom hems, layered over frayed pale-grey or off-white undershirts, and dark grey torn trousers with shredded knee holes.
- Posture: Hunched off-balance postures, thin black stick arms extended straight forward with simplified clawed hands, thin stick legs in slow shuffled lines or dense crowd swarms.

4. SECONDARY SURVIVORS / NPCS:
- Built on the identical 2D stick-figure foundation (round off-white head, dot eyes, straight-line mouth, stick limbs, thick black outlines).
- Distinct flat color clothing fitting their role (e.g. Miller in faded denim-blue jacket #3B5372, store clerk in green apron #2E6B4F).

5. ENVIRONMENTS & LIGHTING:
- Interiors: Department stores, supermarkets, underground maintenance tunnels, stockrooms. Flat colored rectangular blocks representing inventory, illuminated by flat white rectangular ceiling fluorescent lights or conical yellow flashlight beams.
- Exteriors / Night: Dominant dark navy-purple skies (#12182E) with flat architectural silhouettes and warm localized yellow streetlamp light pools.
