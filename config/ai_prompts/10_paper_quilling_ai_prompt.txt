STYLE DIRECTIVES: PAPER QUILLING ILLUSTRATION
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- A detailed handcrafted paper-quilling illustration aesthetic in which every single visual element is constructed from narrow strips of colored paper (quilling paper) rolled, curled, folded, twisted, layered, and shaped on edge.
- The entire image must look like a genuine, high-relief piece of handmade paper artwork displayed on a sturdy backing board.
- Tangible physical depth: Visible paper edges, rolled coils, curled scrolls, folded surfaces, subtle paper fiber texture, and realistic contact shadows cast by the paper strips under directional lighting.
- Widescreen 16:9 layout (1920x1080).
- STRICTLY AVOID: Photographic textures, ordinary flat digital vector graphics, flat paper drawings, clay textures, 3D CGI materials, or backgrounds that do not look paper-crafted.

2. HUMAN CHARACTERS & FIGURES:
- Humans are constructed through carefully arranged quilling paper components.
- Faces, hair, facial features, clothing, and limbs are formed from tightly rolled, curved, folded, and layered paper strips.
- Hair is formed from cascading curled paper coils; clothing patterns are rendered using decorative paper filigree, concentric loops, and geometric quilling shapes (marquises, tight rolls, loose scrolls).

3. ANIMALS, BIRDS & BOTANICALS:
- Animals retain anatomical clarity while being constructed entirely from shaped paper strips (e.g. fur suggested by layered feathered paper coils, scales by overlapping teardrop quilling shapes).
- Birds feature wings and tail feathers assembled from layered, graduated quilling scrolls.

4. ENVIRONMENTS & OBJECTS:
- Architecture, vehicles, landscapes, clouds, water, and terrain must all be formed from paper-quilling elements.
- The viewer must instantly recognize that every background element, mountain ridge, and cobblestone has been meticulously rolled and glued on edge.
