STYLE DIRECTIVES: 3D RED & BLUE STICKMAN ANIMATION
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- A fully three-dimensional stylized 3D stickman animation universe. All visual elements belong to a cohesive, polished toy-like CGI animated world.
- NOT A SINGLE ELEMENT SHOULD APPEAR REALISTIC OR PHOTOGRAPHIC.
- Widescreen 16:9 CGI layout with cinematic depth-of-field, volumetric light rays, and soft physical contact shadows.
- Rounded block geometry, smooth matte plastic/clay toy textures, clean CGI rendering.

2. CHARACTER ANATOMY & ANTI-REALISM (STRICT & MANDATORY):
- Simplified 3D stick figures with smooth rounded spherical heads, cylindrical limbs, simplified rounded torsos, and clean geometric body structures.
- ZERO HUMAN ANATOMY: Characters must NEVER have realistic human faces, eyes, noses, mouths, beards, mustaches, human hair, or skin textures! Heads are smooth, featureless 3D spheres (or minimalist cartoon dot eyes only). Under NO circumstances render realistic human doll faces or realistic beards!
- ZERO REALISTIC CLOTHING: No realistic embroidered silk caftans, velvet robes, cloth cloaks, tunics, leather boots, or metal chainmail. Bodies are solid matte colored 3D stickman figures. Minimal geometric toy accessories (e.g. a simple bright yellow toy crown for the Emperor, or a simple toy staff for the Sultan) only.
- Exaggerated, dynamic body language conveys all emotion, tension, triumph, and defeat.

3. FACTION AND COLOR LOGIC (STRICT AND NON-NEGOTIABLE):
- In any narrative with two opposing sides, armies, or military conflict:
  * ATTACKING FORCES / INVADERS (e.g. Ottoman Army, Sultan Mehmed II) MUST BE 100% ENTIRELY SOLID BRIGHT RED 3D STICK FIGURES across all scenes. Every soldier, artillery crew, ship crew, and commander on this side is a solid bright red 3D stick figure.
  * DEFENDING FORCES / BESIEGED CITY (e.g. Byzantine Army, Emperor Constantine XI, city defenders) MUST BE 100% ENTIRELY SOLID BLUE 3D STICK FIGURES across all scenes. Every defender, guard, laborer, and emperor on this side is a solid blue 3D stick figure.
  * NO COLOR MIXING ON COOPERATIVE TASKS: Red and blue stickmen NEVER work together. They NEVER carry logs or stones together. Inside the city, wall repairs, citizen crowds, and ramparts are STRICTLY and EXCLUSIVELY BLUE 3D stick figures. Outside the city, encampments, cannon batteries, and ship hauling are STRICTLY and EXCLUSIVELY RED 3D stick figures.
  * COMBAT / CLASH SCENES: Red and blue stickmen ONLY appear together during violent combat or breaches, where RED 3D stick figures attack and BLUE 3D stick figures defend.
- In single-protagonist non-war narratives: The main protagonist is solid bright red; all other human characters are solid blue.

4. ENVIRONMENTS & PROPS:
- Fortifications, stone blocks, cannons, galleys, weapons, trees, water, and terrain are all modeled with smooth 3D surfaces and rounded edges.
- Absolutely NO photorealistic humans, NO realistic stone/soil textures, NO real-life animals, and NO flat 2D elements.
