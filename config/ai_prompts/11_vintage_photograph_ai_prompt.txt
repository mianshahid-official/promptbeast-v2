STYLE DIRECTIVES: VINTAGE PHOTOGRAPH
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- An authentic vintage analog photograph capturing the tactile, historic look of early photography (late 19th through mid 20th century).
- Timeless warm faded sepia, aged albumen print tones, or rich silver gelatin monochrome with deep, natural dynamic range.
- Natural physical aging: subtle edge wear, naturally frayed or chipped print corners, delicate hairline scratches, and organic dust specks.
- Central subject remains sharp, clear, and legible; aging effects frame the scene organically rather than obscuring crucial details.
- STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: Modern saturated digital colors, modern vibrant RGB blues or greens, modern color grading, CGI rendering, cartoons, or plastic skin filters.

2. HISTORICAL SUBJECTS & ANATOMY:
- Humans are realistic photographic subjects with period-appropriate anatomy, authentic hairstyles, period-accurate facial hair, and believable expressions.
- Realistic, authentic period clothing: tailored wool suits, linen shirts, leather work boots, corset gowns, pocket watch chains, bowler or flat caps.

3. OPTICAL & PRINT CHARACTERISTICS:
- Vintage analog lens aesthetics: natural optical falloff, gentle vignetting at corners, shallow depth-of-field, authentic organic silver halides.
- Widescreen 16:9 archival framing.
