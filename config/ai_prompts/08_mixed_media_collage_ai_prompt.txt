================================================================================
STYLE DIRECTIVES: MIXED-MEDIA COLLAGE
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- A sophisticated mixed-media collage illustration aesthetic combining photographs, printed illustrations, newspaper clippings, archival documents, textured paper, hand-drawn elements, cutout subjects, and graphic fragments into one cohesive editorial composition.
- The visual piece must look like a carefully assembled physical or fine-art digital collage with tactile physical depth, subtle paper edge drop shadows, and distinct material textures.
- Widescreen 16:9 editorial layout with strong visual hierarchy and clear focal points.
- AVOID: Plain photorealism without collage treatment, chaotic cluttered designs without focal points, excessive random decorative lettering, and conflicting cartoon elements.

2. HUMAN CHARACTERS:
- Humans appear as photographic cutouts, illustrated portraits, printed figures, or an intentional combination.
- Recognizable, anatomically believable, with visible cut paper edges, layered outlines, or printed halftone ink patterns.
- Consistent recurring character identity across scenes (same facial cutout or portrait style).

3. ANIMALS, BIRDS & OBJECTS:
- Animals and birds retain recognizable species-specific anatomy, represented through vintage photographic cutouts, engraved naturalist prints, or hand-drawn collage elements.
- Architecture, vehicles, machinery, and objects are assembled from vintage blueprints, architectural line drawings, and photographic cutout fragments.

4. MATERIALS, TEXTURES & SCRIPT INTEGRATION:
- Visible paper layers, deckled and torn borders, printed ink halftones, aged parchment, newsprint typography, map fragments, and faint hand-drawn charcoal or ink marks.
- Materials must directly support the narration (e.g. military battle maps for war scenes, financial ledgers for economic themes, botanical sketches for nature topics).
