STYLE DIRECTIVES: 3D STICKMAN STYLE
================================================================================

1. GLOBAL VISUAL FOUNDATION:
- 3D CGI hybrid cartoon visual identity blending OverSimplified animation geometry with high-end, tactile 3D rendering.
- Converts minimalist flat cartoon storytelling into a fully realized, three-dimensional physical world with clean shapes and tactile depth.
- Smooth, soft-toy low-poly modeling, matte surface finishes, vibrant color saturation, and a polished animated feature look.
- Cinematic 16:9 widescreen composition with dynamic depth-of-field, subtle ambient occlusion, and soft physical contact shadows.
- STRICTLY AVOID: Photorealistic human skin pores, hyper-detailed facial muscle anatomy, flat 2D vector art, harsh digital noise, grimdark blood/gore, hyper-realistic hair systems, or conflicting art styles.

2. HUMAN SUBJECTS & ANATOMY:
- Stylized 3D stickman characters featuring iconic smooth spherical heads, minimal or absent nose/ear geometry, and simplified rounded cylindrical bodies.
- Facial expressions rely on high-contrast black dot eyes, simple line mouth poses, and animated eyebrow shifts rather than complex muscle topology.
- Clothing adapts accurately to the story (period coats, survival flannel, armor, workwear), modeled with simplified cloth folds, matte fabric textures, and clean color blocking that preserve lightweight stickman proportions.

3. ENVIRONMENT & ARCHITECTURE:
- Fully modeled, dimensional 3D environments that strictly honor the story setting facts.
- Buildings, terrain, vehicles, and props are rendered with simplified architectural geometry and soft, tactile material textures (weathered wood grain, smooth stone masonry, matte metal, stylized foliage).
- High environmental depth, clean background separation, and toy-like structural physics.

4. LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE:
- Dynamic cinematic lighting with soft physical shadows, subtle ambient occlusion, and warm bounce light.
- Atmospheric effects such as golden-hour sunlight, soft volumetric haze, campfire glows, or snow flurries add natural depth and mood without cluttering character clarity.
