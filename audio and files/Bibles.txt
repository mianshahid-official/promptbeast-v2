1. CHARACTER BIBLE

HERO NARRATOR ("YOU")

Role & Identity:

- Main protagonist and narrator of the zombie survival story.
- A resourceful survivor trapped inside a massive shopping mall during the outbreak.
- Strategic, observant, cautious and highly disciplined.
- Relies on planning, resource management, stealth and controlled defensive actions rather than unnecessary confrontation.

Anatomy & Head Proportions:

- Standardized 2D stick-figure anatomy.
- Large round off-white head (#F5F5F0), approximately one-third of total body height.
- Two small solid black circular eyes.
- Short, straight black mouth.
- No nose, ears or realistic facial anatomy.
- Short black hair tufts visible around the forehead and behind the ears.
- Thin black stick arms and legs with simple joints and minimal extremities.

Locked Wardrobe & Colors:

- Backwards flat-red baseball cap (#D63A2A).
- Dark curved cap brim pointing behind the head.
- Small black adjustment strap detail at the front brow.
- Dark charcoal-grey zip-up hoodie (#333333).
- Pure-white undershirt (#FFFFFF).
- Dark brown cross-body messenger bag strap (#4A2E1B), running from right shoulder to left hip.
- Small silver key clip attached to the waistband.

Important Props & Tools:

- Box cutter.
- Clipboard.
- Yellow survival notepad.
- Flashlight.
- Crowbar.
- Water containers.
- Survival backpack.
- Metal security keys.
- Small inventory lists and maps.

Visual Expression & Demeanor:

- Neutral straight-line mouth during routine activities.
- Slightly lowered head when concentrating.
- Wide dot eyes when surprised or alarmed.
- Controlled body posture during dangerous situations.
- Avoid exaggerated facial expressions.

Visual Continuity Rules:

- The backwards red cap must always remain visible.
- Hoodie and messenger bag strap colors never change.
- Head proportions and stick anatomy remain identical.
- The hero never develops realistic facial features.
- Props must appear only when relevant to the current scene.
- The hero must look identical across all 60 days of the story.

MILLER — SECONDARY SURVIVOR

Role & Identity:

- Delivery driver trapped inside the mall loading bay during the initial lockdown.
- Survives alone for 42 days before encountering the hero.
- Becomes the second human survivor in the fortified mall.
- Emotionally vulnerable, talkative under pressure and deeply attached to his daughter.

Anatomy & Head Proportions:

- Round off-white stick-figure head (#F5F5F0).
- Two small black dot eyes.
- Simple straight-line mouth.
- Thin black stick arms and legs.
- Same standardized proportions as the hero.

Locked Wardrobe & Colors:

- Faded denim-blue jacket (#3B5372).
- Pale-grey undershirt.
- Dark charcoal trousers.
- Simple dark footwear represented through flat shapes.
- No cap or cross-body messenger bag.

Important Props & Tools:

- Metal pipe.
- Delivery backpack.
- Five-gallon water jug.
- Daughter's framed photograph.
- Paperback book.
- Small personal belongings.

Visual Expression & Demeanor:

- Nervous posture with slightly hunched shoulders.
- Frequently moving arms while speaking.
- Shaking posture during moments of extreme fear.
- Protective grip on his daughter's photograph.
- Later scenes show a tired, injured survivor with limited mobility.

Visual Continuity Rules:

- Blue jacket remains identical throughout the story.
- Miller's face and body proportions never change.
- His daughter's photograph must remain visually consistent.
- His calf injury appears after the Day 57 glass accident.
- The injury remains bandaged throughout the remaining scenes.
- Miller cannot walk normally after the injury.

- Unblinking circular eyes.
- Hunched posture.

4. CINEMATIC STYLE BIBLE

AESTHETIC DEFINITION

The entire production uses pure flat 2D vector cartoon artwork.

Mandatory visual characteristics:

- Flat solid-color fills.
- Heavy uniform near-black outlines (#1A1A1A).
- Simple stick-figure anatomy.
- Geometric architectural backgrounds.
- Minimal facial details.
- Consistent character proportions.
- Clear silhouettes.
- Clean vector-style shapes.

Strictly prohibited:

- 3D rendering.
- Photorealism.
- CGI effects.
- Gradients.
- Volumetric lighting.
- Realistic skin textures.
- Realistic anatomy.
- Graphic blood or gore.
- Detailed wounds.
- Inconsistent character designs.

7\. FINAL STORY VISUAL IDENTITY

CORE VISUAL IDENTITY

A pure flat 2D cartoon zombie-survival world set inside an enormous modern shopping mall after a sudden outbreak.

The visual storytelling revolves around three central elements:

- The hero: A consistent stick-figure survivor wearing a backwards red cap and charcoal-grey hoodie, relying on planning, discipline and limited resources.
- The mall: A once-bright commercial environment gradually transforming into a dark, abandoned and partially destroyed survival environment.
- The horde: Identical olive-green infected figures with torn brown jackets, white circular eyes and jagged teeth, representing the constant danger outside the fortified shelter.

THEMATIC IDENTITY

The story is about resource management, controlled movement, environmental awareness, trust and the limitations of large defensive spaces.

The mall begins as a place of comfort and abundance but gradually becomes a dangerous maze of darkness, abandoned supplies and infected crowds.

The sporting goods store becomes a smaller fortress inside a much larger structure. Its security depends not on fighting every zombie but on limiting access, controlling noise, protecting resources and maintaining silence.

FINAL VISUAL MESSAGE

The ground floor represents everything lost. The second floor represents the small amount of control the survivors managed to preserve.

The final image should show the hero and Miller inside their dark, fortified sporting goods store, with their remaining supplies nearby and the destroyed mall visible through the peephole.

The world outside has not been defeated. The survivors have simply built a small protected space within it.

Series-wide visual identity: Consistent characters, flat vector environments, heavy black outlines, controlled colors, chronological environmental deterioration and tension created through silence, darkness and visual storytelling.
